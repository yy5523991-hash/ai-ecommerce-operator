# Platform Matrix

Use this matrix as a starting point, then verify current official rules when exact fees, deposit, category permissions, or entry requirements affect the decision.

| Platform | Beginner Fit | Low-Budget Fit | Traffic Source | Margin Potential | Main Risk | Default Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---|---|
| Xiaohongshu | High | Medium | Search + recommendation + lifestyle content | High | Content trust and compliance wording | Best default for no-face, high-margin niche goods |
| Douyin Shop | Medium | Medium | Short video + livestream + paid traffic | Medium | Video output pressure and traffic volatility | Use only if user can produce frequent short videos |
| Taobao | Medium | Medium | Search + ads + store operations | Medium | Cold-start search and paid competition | Use for standardized goods with search demand |
| Pinduoduo | Medium | High | Price recommendation | Low | Price war and after-sales pressure | Avoid for high-margin beginner strategy |
| WeChat Store | Low at start | High later | Private traffic | High | No public traffic by default | Use after private traffic exists |

## Platform Selection Scoring

Score each platform from 1 to 5:

- Startup cost
- Natural traffic access
- No-face content fit
- Gross margin room
- Low compliance complexity
- Low fulfillment complexity

Choose the highest score. If two platforms tie, choose the one with lower fulfillment and compliance risk.

## Default Xiaohongshu Conditions

Choose Xiaohongshu when:

- User is a solo beginner.
- Budget is under 5000 RMB.
- User can make graphic notes but does not want to appear on camera.
- Products are visual, scene-based, problem-solving, lightweight, and not highly regulated.
- Gross margin can stay above 45% after product cost, shipping, platform fees, and after-sales buffer.

Do not choose Xiaohongshu as the only channel when:

- Product needs aggressive lowest-price competition.
- Product needs professional qualification the user does not have.
- User cannot make basic scene photos or comparison images.
- Product is hard to explain visually.

## Safe Category Bias

Prefer: storage, desktop organization, travel accessories, car interior storage/cleaning accessories, stationery, gift packaging, small home convenience items.

Avoid for beginners: food, supplements, medicine, medical devices, cosmetics with strong efficacy claims, electrical appliances with certification uncertainty, children/baby safety-critical items, branded/IP goods, pet food, high-breakage glass/ceramic goods, heavy furniture.
