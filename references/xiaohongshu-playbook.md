# Xiaohongshu Closed-Loop Playbook

Use this reference when the selected platform is Xiaohongshu.

## Store Setup

1. Choose a personal/solo-friendly store type if allowed by current rules.
2. Prepare ID, bank card, phone number, return address, and category qualification if required.
3. Name the store with `人群/场景 + 类目 + 店铺感`, such as `租房收纳研究所`, `桌面秩序铺`, `出差收纳小店`.
4. Avoid names or claims implying official status, flagship status, guaranteed results, medical effect, or absolute superiority.
5. Start with 10 candidate SKUs, list 3 first, publish 7-14 notes, then keep only winners.

## 1688 Product Filters

Reject a product if any condition is true:

- Gross margin after cost and shipping is below 45%.
- Supplier does not support one-piece dropshipping or small-batch testing.
- Recent reviews mention `异味`, `掉色`, `断裂`, `漏发`, `尺寸不准`, `做工差`, or `客服差` repeatedly.
- Product is over 1kg without a clear high selling price.
- Product needs certification the seller cannot verify.
- Main image or detail image authorization is unclear and the user cannot shoot their own images.

Prefer products with:

- Source factory, strong merchant, buyer protection, 48-hour shipping.
- Clear SKU dimensions and color options.
- Monthly sales with stable reviews rather than sudden abnormal spikes.
- Simple after-sales: replacement/refund can be handled without technical diagnosis.
- Bundle potential: 2-piece, 3-piece, color set, scene set.

## Pricing Formula

Use:

`target_price = (cost + shipping + packaging + platform_fee_estimate + after_sales_buffer) / (1 - target_margin)`

Beginner default:

- Target gross margin: 50%-65%
- After-sales buffer: 5%-8%
- First price test: cost-to-selling-price multiplier 2.5-3.5
- Coupon: 3-10 RMB
- Bundle threshold: 39/59/99 RMB depending on category

## No-Face Content Framework

Title:

`我后悔没早点买这个XX，真的把XX救回来了`

Body:

```text
之前我的【场景】一直很乱，尤其是【具体痛点】。
后来换成这个【产品】，最明显的变化是：

1. 【变化1】
2. 【变化2】
3. 【变化3】

适合：【人群/场景】
不适合：【边界条件】
尺寸/颜色：【规格】

我放在店铺了，拿不准尺寸的可以直接问我。
```

Seven images:

1. Cover: pain point + product result.
2. Messy scene before use.
3. Before/after comparison.
4. Material and detail close-up.
5. Installation or use steps.
6. SKU color/size comparison.
7. Buying guide and after-sales note.

## Customer-Service Templates

Welcome:

`亲，欢迎来店里～这款是【产品】，适合【场景】。需要我帮你看尺寸/颜色吗？`

Recommendation:

`如果你是【轻度需求】，建议选【规格A】；如果东西多，直接选【规格B】，更稳也更划算。`

Price:

`亲，这款现在是小店活动价，质量和售后我会把关。你可以先领【优惠券】，到手更合适。`

Shipping:

`亲，正常【时效】内发出，发出后系统会自动同步单号。`

Out of stock:

`亲，这个颜色暂时缺货，预计【时间】补。你可以换【替代款】，我优先给你安排发货。`

Logistics delay:

`亲我马上帮你查物流。如果超过正常时效，我会同步催快递，有结果第一时间告诉你。`

Quality issue:

`抱歉影响体验了。麻烦拍一下外包装和问题位置，我核实后给你安排补发/换货/退款。`

Return/exchange:

`亲可以按平台流程申请退换，商品保持完整、不影响二次销售即可。我这边看到申请会尽快处理。`

Negative feedback:

`亲真的抱歉，这次体验没做好。你方便说下具体问题吗？我优先给你处理到满意。`

Repeat purchase:

`亲你上次买的是【产品A】，如果还要搭配，可以看【产品B】，尺寸是配套的。`

| 5 min | Record next action | Tomorrow's first task |

## Seven-Day Sprint

Day 1: Find 10 products and reject weak items.

Day 2: Ask suppliers for dropshipping, image authorization, shipping time, and return rules.

Day 3: List 3 products with 7-image structure.

Day 4-6: Publish 1 note per day per product angle.

Day 7: Keep products with saves or inquiries. Replace zero-click products.
